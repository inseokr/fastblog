When should the falling photos appear?

The current creation animation runs for 5 seconds. Here are 3 timing options for the falling-photos finale.

A

Last 2 seconds of the existing 5s window

At ~3s, the rings/progress UI fades out and the falling photos kick in for the final 2 seconds. Total animation time stays at 5s. Clean & fast — no extra wait added.

Rings + progress (3s)
Photos fall (2s)
→ Blog reveals
|---- 5s total ----|
B

After the 5s animation finishes (extended finale)

Full 5s animation plays as-is. Once the blog is ready, the rings fade out and the falling photos play as a dedicated ~2.5s finale before revealing the blog. Total: ~7.5s. More dramatic, but adds wait time.

Rings + progress (5s)
Photos fall (2.5s)
→ Blog
|-------- ~7.5s total --------|
C

Overlay: photos fall on top of the existing animation

At ~3s, the photos begin falling while the rings are still faintly visible underneath. Both layers coexist briefly, then a flash transition reveals the blog. Adds depth — photos feel like they emerge from the app icon.

Rings + progress (fades to 30% at 3s)
Photos fall on top
→ Flash → Blog
|---- 5s total ----|

Note: In all options, the actual blog data finishes loading asynchronously — the animation waits for it regardless of which timing approach is used.
