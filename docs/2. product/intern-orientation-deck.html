Key Technologies
How Bloggo actually works.
Three layers of intelligence turn your raw photo library into a structured, beautiful blog — all on-device, all automatic.

  • On-Device AI/ML: Extracts sentiment, quality scores, and metadata from photos
  • Smart POI Detection: Automatically finds and names the places you visited
  • Blog Generation: Assembles RecapBlogDetail with ranked photos and narrative

The Codebase
MVVM — four strict layers.
Every file in Bloggo lives in one of four layers. Understanding the boundary is your most important first job here.
  • Views never call Services directly — always through a ViewModel
  • ViewModels are @MainActor — all @Published updates are thread-safe
  • Services are singletons — use .shared, don't recreate them
  • Models are plain structs — Codable, Equatable, Identifiable
Not sure where code belongs?
Ask: "does this know about the UI?" → push it down a layer.
