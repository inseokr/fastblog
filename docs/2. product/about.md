## What is Bloggo

Bloggo is an AI-powered travel journal iOS app that automatically turns your camera roll photos into beautiful, shareable blogs — organized by day and place, with AI-generated captions and narratives. No manual sorting. No blank page. You take the photos; Bloggo builds the story.

It operates in two modes: **Trip Blogs** for multi-day travel, and **My Places** for everyday moments near home. The output can be exported as a blog, PDF, video reel, carousel, or storybook. Everything is stored privately on device — blog content never leaves unless the user chooses to share it.

Bloggo is made by **LinkedSpaces LLC**.

## Mission

> **Be the home for your travel life.**

The app you open before a trip to plan it, during a trip to capture it, and after a trip to remember and share it — all without feeling like work.

## Vision

Bloggo's long-term vision is to become the main Social Memory Network Platform that follows a user through every phase of their journey (Daily Moments, Lunch plans, weekend trips, vacation):

- **Before** — AI-powered itinerary builder that learns from your past trips and wishlist
- **During** — Collaborative trip rooms where friends contribute photos in real-time and Bloggo auto-generates one group blog at the end
- **After** — The best post-trip blog generator on iOS: zero manual input, beautifully designed output
- **Beyond** — A travel network where your blogs, places, and stats become a verified, living record of your travel life — and the foundation of a content ecosystem others can build on

Bloggo strives to be the best memory blog generator on iOS. Every feature we build is laying the foundation for that larger platform.

## MVP — The Core Product

The non-negotiable experience Bloggo must always deliver:

1. **Scan** — User grants photo library access; Bloggo finds trips by analyzing date and GPS metadata
2. **Organize** — Photos are grouped by day and place automatically, no manual sorting required
3. **Generate** — AI writes captions and a trip narrative from the organized photos
4. **Export** — The finished blog is shareable as a link, PDF, video reel, carousel, or storybook

If any of these four steps feel like work, the product has failed its core promise.

## Target Audience

### Primary — The Post-Trip Archivist

**Who they are:** Everyday travelers aged 25–45. They take hundreds of photos on every trip — weekend getaways, family holidays, solo adventures. Their camera roll is full. Their intention to "do something with those photos someday" is genuine. They just never get around to it.

**Their habits:** They use Apple Photos or Google Photos as passive storage. They've tried journaling apps but found them too manual. They share highlights to Instagram Stories and forget the rest. Three months after a trip, they can barely remember the names of the places they visited.

**Their relationship to tech:** Comfortable with iOS, not interested in learning tools. They want things to just work.

**What they feel:** Organizing photos after a trip feels like homework. The magic of the experience fades while they're still procrastinating the write-up. They wish they had a beautiful record but don't want to be the person who sits down and makes one.

---

### Secondary — The Everyday Moment-Catcher

**Who they are:** People who aren't always traveling internationally but want to document their everyday local life — favorite coffee shops, new restaurants, neighborhood walks, day trips. They use **My Places** mode.

**Their habits:** They discover places organically and want to remember them. They're not creating content for an audience — they want a personal record they can look back on.

**What they feel:** Standard apps (Maps, Notes) feel too transactional. They want something warmer, more personal, that captures the vibe of a place alongside the photo.

## Pain Points & Solutions

| Pain Point | How Bloggo Solves It |
|---|---|
| "Organizing photos after a trip feels like homework" | Zero manual sorting — Bloggo groups photos by day and place automatically using date + GPS metadata |
| "I can never remember where I went or what it was called" | Every place is named and geolocated in the blog; the narrative gives it context |
| "Journaling apps require too much writing" | AI generates captions and narratives — the user doesn't write a single word unless they want to |
| "I have hundreds of photos but no finished product" | Blog is generated in seconds from the camera roll; no blank page, no manual layout |
| "I'm worried about my private memories being shared or stored somewhere" | Blog content is processed on-device and never uploaded to servers unless the user explicitly shares |
| "The trip felt special but my photos just look like a random album" | Regional visual themes, cinematic design, and narrative structure turn a photo dump into a story |
| "I want to remember everyday places, not just trips" | My Places mode captures local moments with the same quality and organization as trip blogs |

## Core Values

These principles guide every product and design decision at Bloggo.

**1. Effortless over powerful**  
We always choose the path that requires less from the user. The app does the work; the user gets the result. A feature that saves two taps is worth the engineering time.

**2. Privacy is a feature, not a disclaimer**  
Blog content never leaves the device unless the user chooses to share it. We write about this like something the user will be glad to know — not like a legal footnote.

**3. Cinematic quality**  
Every output — blog, PDF, reel, storybook — should feel like a premium publication, not a screenshot. Photography is always the hero. UI is the supporting cast.

**4. Travel-native intelligence**  
We understand trips: days, places, moments, narratives. We don't build generic photo tools with a travel skin. Every decision assumes the user has just come back from somewhere and wants to remember it.

**5. Memories over metrics**  
No follower counts. No likes. No view counts. No performance anxiety. Bloggo is a private space for the user's own travel life. We never add social pressure mechanics.

**6. Honest copy, always**  
We say what the app does in plain English. Warm, not hype. Specific, not vague. If something goes wrong, we say what happened and what the user should do — never "something went wrong."

## What Bloggo Is NOT

Explicit guardrails. These are directions we intentionally avoid — understanding why is as important as knowing what we build.

| What | Why we don't build it |
|---|---|
| A social feed with follower counts | Turns the product into an influencer platform; creates performance anxiety that kills the private, personal feel |
| A booking or planning tool (flights, hotels) | Tripadvisor's territory; we'd be a worse version. Bloggo documents; it doesn't transact |
| A review platform (star ratings, public comments) | Ratings invite negativity and gaming. Public comments before community maturity = toxic or empty |
| A sponsored itinerary tool | Destroys trust in AI recommendations the moment a paid placement appears |
| A web or Android app | iOS-first, done better than anyone. Cross-platform dilutes focus before the core is perfect |
| A tool that uploads your blog content to our servers | Privacy-first is non-negotiable. Blog content stays on device |

## Product Glossary

Canonical terms used across the app, copy, and team communication. Use these — never the alternatives listed.

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| **Blog** | The finished output Bloggo generates from a user's camera roll, or photos taken using our in-app camera | Post, entry, story (unless in "cover story" context) |
| **Reel** | An automated video collage of your footage taken using our in-app camera | Video, clip, short |
| **Vibe** | Ambient audio recorded or selected to accompany a blog | Background audio, soundtrack |
| **Place / Place Stop** | A named location within a blog day | Location, pin, spot |
| **Moment** | A single photo or capture within a Place Stop | Photo, capture |
| **My Places** | The everyday home-area documentation mode | Daily mode, home mode |
| **Trip Blog** | A multi-day travel blog generated from a trip | Travel log, trip |
| **Draft** | A blog that has been scanned but not yet generated | Pending, unfinished |
| **Story / Caption** | AI-generated narrative text within a blog | AI text, generated text |
| **LinkedSpaces LLC** | The company behind Bloggo | Linked Spaces, LS |
