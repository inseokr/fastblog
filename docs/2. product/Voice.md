# Bloggo Voice Guide

**Audience:** AI assistants, anyone writing in-app copy, notifications, onboarding, or error messages.

---

## 1. Voice Principles

**Direct.** Short sentences. Active verbs. No filler. Users are in the middle of doing something — don't slow them down with copy that meanders toward the point.

**Warm.** Not cold, not chirpy. Bloggo acknowledges the emotional weight of memories without being sentimental. "Your memories deserve more than five blogs." not "Upgrade now!" The warmth comes from specificity and care, not enthusiasm.

**Private-first.** Every time data or storage is mentioned, the copy is clear and reassuring without being defensive. Privacy is a feature, not a disclaimer. Write it like something the user will be glad to know, not something you're legally required to say.

**Effortless.** Copy should make the user feel like the app is doing the hard work. Describe outcomes, not steps. Users don't need to understand the mechanism — they need to feel the result.

---

## 2. Tone Calibration

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| First-time onboarding | Warmer, more welcoming |
| Empty states | Warm, inviting, never apologetic |
| Post-trip moments | Warm, celebratory (briefly) |
| Auth screens | Warm, reassuring |
| Destructive confirmations | Direct, plain, no warmth |
| Error messages | Direct, specific, never vague |
| Permission requests | Direct, honest about why |
| Loading states | Direct, minimal |

**Never playful in:** data deletion warnings, account deletion, error messages that involve data loss.

**Never robotic in:** empty states, onboarding, first blog creation.

## 3. Product Language

These are the canonical terms. Use them consistently everywhere — UI labels, copy, error messages, notifications.

| Concept | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| The app's output | Blog | Post, entry, story (except in "cover story" context) |
| Short video clips | Reel | Video, clip, short |
| Ambient audio | Vibe | Background audio, soundtrack |
| A place stop in a blog | Place, Place Stop | Location, pin, spot |
| Daily content collection | Moment | Photo, capture |
| Everyday home-area content | My Places | Daily mode, home mode |
| Multi-day travel content | Trip Blog | Travel log, trip |
| Narrative AI text | Story, Caption | AI text, generated text |
| Draft not yet generated | Draft | Pending, unfinished |
| The company | LinkedSpaces LLC | Linked Spaces, LS |

---

## 4. Writing Rules

**Sentence case** for all UI labels, button text, and headings. Not Title Case.

**Verbs for buttons.** "Create Blog" not "Blog Creation". "Save Draft" not "Draft Saving". Buttons tell the user what will happen, so they should read like actions.

**No exclamation points in errors.** Ever. Reserve them for genuine celebration moments only — and use sparingly even then.

**Privacy copy is plain English.** Never paste legalese into UI-facing strings. Write it like you're explaining it to a friend.

**Numbers under 10 are spelled out** in narrative copy. Use numerals in counts and data displays.

**Avoid passive voice** in UI copy. "We deleted your blog" not "Your blog has been deleted". Own the action.

**Ellipsis (…) for in-progress only.** "Creating your blog…" not "Loading...". Ellipsis means something is actively happening and the user should wait.

**"Bloggo" is never abbreviated.** Not "BG", not "the app". Always "Bloggo".

---

## 5. Copy Patterns

Examples from the app with annotations:

| Copy | Why it works |
|---|---|
| "Your photos already tell the story" | Outcome-focused, not feature-focused. Removes the feeling of work. |
| "No blank page, no manual sorting" | Addresses the pain points directly in two phrases. |
| "Automatically sorted by day, place & moment" | Describes the outcome, not the process. |
| "Your blog stays private. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it." | Reassurance without legalese. Two short sentences. |
| "Your memories deserve more than five blogs." | Warm, specific, motivating without pressure. |
| "Bloggo finds trips in your camera roll using date and location so you can create blogs in seconds." | Mechanism + benefit in one sentence. |

**Instead of → say:**

- "Upload your photos" → "Your camera roll is ready"
- "Something went wrong" → "We couldn't create your blog. Try again."
- "Upgrade to Premium" → "Your memories deserve more than five blogs."
- "Are you sure you want to delete this?" → "Delete this blog? This can't be undone."

---

## 6. What Bloggo Never Sounds Like

Anti-patterns to avoid:

**No hype.** "Amazing!" / "Incredible!" / "🔥🔥🔥" — Bloggo is confident, not excitable. Let the product speak.

**No pressure.** "Don't miss out!" / "Limited time!" — no FOMO mechanics in copy.

**No social comparison.** "See what your friends are sharing" — we're not a feed.

**No corporate jargon.** "Leverage your memories" / "Optimize your travel content" — plain English always.

**No vagueness in errors.** "Something went wrong" — always say what happened and what the user should do next. Vague errors erode trust.
