# Bloggo — Social & Fun Features Vision

> **For interns:** These are the features we're building next to make Bloggo something people *share* and *use together*, not just solo. Each one is a real opportunity to own a feature end-to-end.

---

## 1. Shared Trip Room ⭐ (Priority)

**"Start a trip like you'd start a Partiful event."**

One person creates a Trip Room before a group trip. Friends join via a link or QR code. As everyone takes photos during the trip, they flow into a shared live feed — like a group photo album that builds itself in real-time. When the trip ends and everyone's home, Bloggo auto-detects it and generates **one beautiful group blog** from all the photos combined.

Each person owns and can edit only the content they contributed. The result is a single shared blog the whole group can look back on.

**Why it drives downloads:** Your friend creates a room and sends you a link. You download Bloggo to join. That's organic, word-of-mouth growth built into the core feature.

**Intern opportunity:** Room creation flow, join via deep link, live photo feed UI, auto-detection of trip end.

---

## 2. Trip Invite Card

**"Send a vibe before the trip even starts."**

When you create a Trip Room, Bloggo generates a shareable card — beautiful, auto-designed with the destination name, dates, and a map pin. You send it in iMessage or post it to your story. Friends tap it, join the room, and are ready to contribute photos from day one.

Think: Partiful's event page, but for travel.

**Intern opportunity:** Card design system, share sheet integration, deep link handling.

---

## 3. Live Trip Feed

**"Watch the trip build in real-time."**

Inside a Trip Room, there's a live scrolling feed of every photo added by every member — organized by time and place, auto-labeled with location. You see your friend's photo from the same hike you were both on, taken 10 minutes apart.

Members can react to each other's photos with quick emoji reactions. No commenting, no social pressure — just a lightweight "I saw this" acknowledgment.

**Intern opportunity:** Real-time photo feed UI, reaction system, location-based grouping.

---

## 4. "My Perspective" Split

**"One trip, two angles."**

After the shared blog is generated, each member can tap "See My Perspective" to get a version of the blog filtered to only their photos — auto-generated captions, their own narrative. One trip, personalized for everyone who was there.

Great for groups where some people went to different spots on the same trip.

**Intern opportunity:** Blog filtering logic, per-user narrative generation, split-view UI.

---

## 5. Trip Recap Notification

**"Your trip is ready."**

When Bloggo detects the trip has ended, every member of the room gets a push notification: *"Your trip to Grand Canyon is ready — see the blog."* Opening it reveals the fully generated group blog for the first time. The reveal moment is a shared experience — everyone gets it at the same time.

**Intern opportunity:** Trip-end detection heuristic, coordinated push notification timing, reveal animation.

---

## 6. Shared Blog Link (Read-Only)

**"Show anyone the trip, not just the group."**

The finished group blog can be shared as a public read-only link — like a mini travel website. Anyone with the link can browse it on their phone, even without the app. The link has a soft "Download Bloggo" CTA at the bottom.

This is the biggest download driver: your blog reaches people who weren't on the trip.

**Intern opportunity:** Web view rendering, public share link backend, app CTA integration.
